Privacy policy

1MCP is a personal tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar so his AI assistants can read and manage them on his computer.

No data is collected from anyone else, sold, or shared with third parties. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: open an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
